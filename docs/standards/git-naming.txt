**Issue**:             kebabcase; ex: github-actions-for-linting

↓ 
**branch**:            `build/ || feature/ || bugfix/ || chore/ + <issue-name>_<id>`; ex: build/github-actions-for-linting_4

↓ 
**commit**:            `fix(#<id>) || feat(#<id>) || chore(#<id>) + : + <issue-task> ?? anything`; ex: chore(#4): adding git naming conventions

↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ...
**Pull Request**:      `merge__<branch-name>`; ex: merge__build/github-actions-for-linting_4
